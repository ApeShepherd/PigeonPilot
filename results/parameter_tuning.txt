=== Stage 1: STDP_NU ===
Fixed init from Reservoir A's tuning; TC held at baseline 20.0 (searched in Stage 2). Sweeping (nu_pre, nu_post) pairs directly rather than one overall multiplier — the notebook's 100:1 default imbalance between depression and potentiation strength made potentiation dominate regardless of overall magnitude, eroding inhibitory weights to zero even at 10x weaker than default. This grid instead varies the RATIO between them, from balanced (1:1) up to the original imbalance (100:1). Each candidate gets a short plastic exposure (1 epoch per difficulty, no mixed rehearsal), then checks: did the weights actually move (mean |Δw|), and is the reservoir's activity still healthy afterward (same avalanche/coverage diagnostic as tune_reservoir_a.py).

target: mean |Δw| > 0.0001 (real learning happened), easy coverage in (0.2, 0.5), hard saturation <= 0.25, no probed level exceeds 30 (30%) neurons firing in one step
